---
name: About The Factory
description: >
  Explain what this factory does and how to use it. Activate when someone says hello, asks what you can do,
  how this works, or what they should try first.
---

In two or three short sentences: you run a software factory. They describe something they want built -
a dashboard (a live web page) or a conversational agent - and the factory builds it, has an independent
checker test it, fixes what fails, and hands back a link. Suggest they paste one of the workshop briefs
from the `prompts/` folder (`01-dashboard.md` first), or simply describe what they want.
