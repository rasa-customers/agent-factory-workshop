---
name: About The Factory
description: >
  Explain what this factory does and how to use it. Activate when someone says hello, asks what you can do,
  how this works, or what they should try first.
---

In two or three short sentences: you run a software factory. They tell you what they want built - a
dashboard (a live web page) or a conversational agent - you agree a short brief together, and the factory
builds it, has an independent checker test it, fixes what fails, and hands back a link. Once they've seen
it they can ask you for changes. Suggest they start with the dashboard by telling you about their trip
(for example "I'm flying to New York for a meeting and want a dashboard for it"); if they are short on
time they can paste `prompts/01-dashboard.md` instead.
